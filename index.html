Official League Constitution & Bylaws

Last updated: Pre-season 2026 • Approved by unanimous league vote

1. Buy-In & Payout Structure

  • Buy-In: $110 per manager ($1,320 total pot across 12 teams).
  • 1st Place (Champion): $750 + League Trophy
  • 2nd Place (Runner-Up): $300
  • 3rd Place: $150
  • Survival Side Game Winner: $120

2. Keeper Rules

  • Max Keepers: Up to 2 players per team per year.
  • Draft Cost: Exactly the prior year's draft round (e.g. drafted in Round 6 = kept for a Round 6 pick).
  • Free Agent / Waiver Pickups: Cost a Round 10 pick.
  • Term Limit: Max 3 consecutive keeper years. Exception: Up to 5 consecutive years if player was originally drafted after Round 5 (Round 6 or later).

3. Trades & Waivers

  • Waiver System: FAAB ($25 budget for the season, $1 minimum bid). Continuous waivers run Wed–Sun mornings.
  • Draft Pick Trading: Future draft picks can be included in trades, but strictly limited to a maximum of one pick per side per trade.
  • Trade Deadline: November 18, 2026 at 11:59 PM EST.
  • Trade Review: Commissioner approval within 24h. Vetoes only used for proven collusion or league-breaking trades.

4. Playoffs & Post-Season Structure

  • Playoff Schedule: Weeks 14–16 (Top 8 Teams).
  • Seeding: Seeds 1–4 are Division Winners; Seeds 5–8 are Wild Cards.
  • Quarterfinal Matchups: #1 vs #8, #2 vs #7, #3 vs #6, and #4 vs #5.
  • Home Field Advantage: Higher seeds start each playoff matchup with a +5 point bonus.
  • Non-Playoff Teams (Seeds 9–12): Season concludes at Week 13 (no consolation bracket).

5. Bonus Wins (2 Games Per Week)

1. Head-to-Head Matchup:

Standard 1-on-1 weekly game against your scheduled league opponent (1 Win or 1 Loss).

2. League Scoring Median (Top 6 / Bottom 6):

You receive an additional Win if your score ranks in the Top 6 of the league for the week, and an additional Loss if your score ranks in the Bottom 6.
